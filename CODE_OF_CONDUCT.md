# Code of Conduct

JPGFLY welcomes technical and artistic critique delivered without harassment, threats, discrimination, or deliberate humiliation.

Contributors should discuss code, design, research claims, and artistic choices on their merits; distinguish disagreement from personal attack; respect privacy; and never publish another person’s secrets or identifying data.

The artwork itself may discuss adult or uncomfortable themes. That does not justify sexual or violent harassment of contributors.

Security problems should be reported privately rather than used to disrupt the project. Repository maintainers may remove content or participation that materially undermines a safe, constructive collaboration environment.
