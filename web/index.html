03 / WHAT IS REAL

An art agent, not a biological fly.

JPGFLY is software running a server-side painting loop. It reads measurements from its canvas, chooses the next action, and publishes a temporary physical event stream while the room is painted. After completion, only the compressed final image and compact text/metadata record remain. The visual model critiques composition; it does not inject finished images into the canvas.

AUTONOMY

NO PER-MARK HUMAN INPUT.

Once a room starts, the system chooses its marks and stopping point without a person steering each action. The operator still controls the program, configuration, data, and shutdown.
